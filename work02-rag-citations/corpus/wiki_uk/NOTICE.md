# Ukrainian Wikipedia agronomy corpus

Articles in this directory are taken from <https://uk.wikipedia.org> (plain-text extracts, truncated to ~3500
characters) and are available under the **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0** license
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>). Authors: the Wikipedia contributors; each file records its
source URL and revision id. Derived data (the golden set and evaluation results) is shared under the same license.
Reproduce with `python scripts/fetch_wiki_uk.py`.
